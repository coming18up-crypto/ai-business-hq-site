AI Business HQ — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

Overview

AI Business HQ is a productivity application for managing content and authorized social media integrations.

Information We Access

With user authorization, AI Business HQ may access TikTok account identifiers, profile information, account statistics, video information, and permissions necessary to upload or publish content.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to connect authorized accounts, display relevant account information, manage content workflows, and support user-authorized publishing.

Data Sharing

AI Business HQ does not sell TikTok user data. Information may be transmitted to TikTok as necessary to provide authorized integration features.

Data Security

We use reasonable security measures to protect account credentials and access tokens.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as necessary to provide the service. Users may request deletion of their connected account data through the service operator.

User Control

Users can revoke TikTok access through their TikTok account settings.

Updates

This policy may be updated as the application changes.
